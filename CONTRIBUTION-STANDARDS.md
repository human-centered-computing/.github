# Human Contribution Standards

Human effort is recognized through evidence, not activity volume. This implements [POLICIES.md](POLICIES.md) and the existing contribution guide.

## Recognize contributions

Record ideas, requirements, architecture, software, design, research, documentation, translation, testing, security review, mentorship, maintenance and user-impact validation. A person who uploads a change is not necessarily its author. Distinguish original ideas, subsequent contributions, AI assistance and independent verification.

Each contribution record has a stable ID, project/task, actor and role, contribution type, description, applicable evidence (PR, design, test, decision or user feedback reference), attribution, review status, reviewer and correction history. Public attribution must respect privacy and permissions; restricted evidence stays restricted.

## Evaluate value

Use contextual evidence across technical quality, human benefit, knowledge sharing, collaboration and maintainability. Define baseline and criteria before evaluation where possible. Completing tasks or generating commits does not establish human benefit. Do not infer low contribution from low commit count or AI use. Record uncertainty and disagreements; allow the contributor to inspect, correct and appeal records. No automated ranking, punitive surveillance, or high-stakes personnel decision based solely on this data.

NextDeveloper will collect software-development contribution evidence; NextContribution remains the planned shared attribution and verification system. NextResult evaluates actual human impact independently of delivery activity. Integrations are proposed, not operational.

## Review

Only authorized reviewers can verify records; authors cannot verify their own disputed claims. Preserve original contribution history and applicable intellectual-property rights. Avoid unnecessary personal data, especially in public Issues or immutable logs.
